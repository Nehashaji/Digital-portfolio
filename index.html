About Me

I am a First Class Honours graduate in BSc (Hons) Creative Computing from Bath Spa University RAK. Throughout my undergraduate studies, I explored a broad range of technologies, which gave me a strong creative and technical foundation. Along the way, I discovered a particular interest in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, leading me to specialise in areas such as machine learning, data analytics, and business intelligence.

Through projects in predictive modelling, computer vision, data analysis, and full-stack development, I have gained practical experience designing and implementing data-driven applications. I enjoy exploring how data can be used to solve real-world problems and creating solutions that combine analytical thinking with modern technologies.

I am particularly interested in building intelligent, impactful, and accessible systems that transform data into meaningful insights and practical solutions. I am continually expanding my technical knowledge and enjoy applying data-driven approaches to solve complex problems and create real-world value.
